# Code of Conduct

Breeze follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, give constructive feedback, and do not harass anyone. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who do not follow it.
